How it works

From sign-up to a live institute, without manual setup.

College details

Institute name, code and official contact information are captured first.

Subdomain

The institute chooses the subdomain its staff and students will use to reach their own environment.

Payment

Payment is collected before the institute's environment is created; a partially completed sign-up can always be resumed and retried.

Environment & admin account created

On confirmed payment, the college record, its default roles and its first College Admin account are created together.

Institute operates independently

The College Admin signs in and manages their own staff, students and site from that point on.

Role-based access

Four roles, scoped to what each one is responsible for.

System-wide

Superuser

  • Manages overall product configuration
  • Not tied to any single institute
Institute admin

College Admin

  • Manages their institute's staff, students and settings
  • Created automatically when the institute's account is set up
Institute staff

Teacher

  • Takes attendance, sets assignments, enters results
  • Scoped to assigned subjects and sections
Institute user

Student

  • Views their own attendance, results and notices
  • Accesses study material for enrolled subjects
Secure by design

Your institute's data stays private to your own account.

Every request is checked against your institute and your role before any data is read or written — your staff and students only ever see information that belongs to your institute.

Your institute has its own subdomain institute.yourdomain
Every sign-in is authenticated first session security
Data access is scoped to your institute only no cross-institute visibility
Access is checked against your role, every request not just at login
Security & access control

Session-based authentication with real, implemented controls.

These are controls implemented in the system today — not a general security claim.

Authenticated sessions

Server-side sessions with HTTP-only, SameSite cookies and a bounded session lifetime.

Institution data isolation

Every view and API endpoint filters by your institute — one institute cannot read another's records.

Role-based authorization

Access to each module is checked against the signed-in user's role and institute on every request, not just at login.

CSRF & CORS controls

Cross-site request forgery protection on state-changing requests, with an explicit allowed-origins list for cross-origin API access.

Audit logging

Administrative actions within your institute are recorded and reviewable by your own admin.

Hardened in production

HSTS, secure cookies and forced HTTPS redirection are enabled automatically outside of debug mode.
